# VAPORA 1.2.4 device review

Existing 1.2.3 users update only the plugin; keep all installed presets.

- Compare the blue wave across Character and Morph settings with 1.2.3. Geometry and note-ripple behavior are unchanged.
- Watch the small stars through a full sky crossing. Paths should be steady, with soft sun and edge fades, without collision jumps or dark marks on the solid sun.
- Small/tiny stars should move at 37.5% of the medium/large stars' speed. The large signature keeps its existing travel speed.
- Step through ten Intervals and inspect the green outline/cross/asterisk signatures, especially the capped plus and forked diagonal cross.
- Sweep Thickness: signature rotation and growth remain smooth; waves are unaffected.
- Sweep Brightness and cycle all seven suns; verify particle and signature occlusion.
- Check Shift toggling, screen switching, six-note playback and the densest star field with saved effects.
- Record any device timing or display issues. Host tests do not establish hardware CPU limits.

Audio, presets and saved controls are unchanged. No preset copying, format or reset is part of this update. Boot-up testing is not a required review step for this revision.
